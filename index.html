A dhaba table, kept simple.

Quetta Mehfil Chai Khana runs on the two things a chai khana is judged on: tea that's actually brewed on the boil, and paratha that's rolled and fried to order. No sit-down formality, just a plaza-front spot where the tea keeps coming.

People typically stay 20 to 45 minutes — long enough for a second cup and the gupshup that comes with it.
